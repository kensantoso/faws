== What this means for the deferred --parallel feature ==
Vending is one `aws` subprocess per profile, run serially, and each subprocess
pays Python/botocore startup cost (aws CLI is a Python program) on top of the
fake vendor's own negligible work. At ~265 ms/profile, a filter that resolves to
hundreds of profiles (plausible at this fixture's scale: --all readonly alone
matches ~250 of the 516) takes multiple MINUTES to vend, not seconds — this is
the finding the brief asked this report to make in plain numbers, not a vague
'it might be slow.' A --parallel flag would cut that wall time roughly in
proportion to the worker count for any selection with no mfa_serial profiles in
it (MFA profiles cannot be parallelized: TOTP codes rotate every 30s and cannot
be reused across AssumeRole calls, so they are inherently serial and must stay
last regardless of what --parallel does to the rest of the batch). Given that
this fixture alone can produce multi-hundred-profile broad filters and multi-
minute vend times, --parallel is a real, not theoretical, need at this scale —
the deferral should be revisited, not left indefinitely.
